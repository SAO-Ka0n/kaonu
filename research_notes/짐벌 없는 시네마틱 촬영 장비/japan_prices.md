# Japanese market prices: bodies and stabilized lenses for gimbal-free handheld video (checked 2026-11-10)

Method note: all "新品最安" figures come from 価格.com item pages (kakaku.com/item/K.../) fetched on 2026-11-10 through the r.jina.ai reader. 価格.com lists prices tax-included (税込). The shop name next to each price was not visible in the text render (it is an image), so **国内正規品 vs 並行輸入 could not be confirmed per listing**. Shipping and stock values are read from the cheapest row. "中古最安" is the cheapest row in the 価格.com 中古価格比較 section on the same page. These rows come from used dealers listing on 価格.com. Kitamura and Map Camera do list there, but the dealer names were not visible. Direct fetches of マップカメラ search, キタムラ (shop.kitamura.jp) search and the Map Camera 買取 page either returned 403 or rendered without prices, so dealer-specific used and buyback prices are mostly **gaps**.

## 1. Canon EOS R8 Mark II: existence, price and cashback (verification of claimed figures)

### Takeaway
The R8 Mark II exists. It went on sale in Japan on 2026-10-29. Every previously claimed figure checks out. The Canon Online Shop body price is ¥275,000. On 2026-11-10 the 価格.com body price list ran from ¥215,795 (lowest, in stock, free shipping) up through ¥224,723 and ¥234,555. A ¥20,000 launch cashback applies to purchases made 2026-10-29 to 2027-01-12. The effective body price after cashback is about **¥195,795** at the lowest listing, or ¥227,500 at the Canon store with its 10% member coupon.

## 4. Resale value of a Canon EOS R8 body

### Takeaway
I found no dealer buyback figure (キタムラ or マップカメラ 買取価格) that I could verify. The Map Camera buyback page loads its price with JavaScript, and Kitamura was blocked. Indirect signals:
- Used retail on 価格.com: ¥149,500–161,500 for B/A rank.
- Fujiya Camera retail: ¥160,600 for AB rank.
- Aggregator "max assessment" figures: ¥104,100–120,000.

So an R8 body probably resells for roughly ¥105,000–125,000, with the R8 Mark II launch likely pushing it down.

### Cited Findings
- 価格.com used R8 body: cheapest ¥149,500 (B), then ¥154,000 (B), ¥155,100 (C), ¥157,000 (A); 104 listings — [K0001514862](https://kakaku.com/item/K0001514862/)
- Fujiya Camera used R8, AB rank: ¥160,600 税込 — [フジヤカメラ](https://www.fujiya-camera.co.jp/shop/g/gC2120191180379/)
- Map Camera has buyback detail pages for "EOS R8 ボディ" and "EOS R8 RF24-50 kit" marked ワンプライス買取, a fixed price with no condition selection. The amount did not render — [Map Camera R8 body 買取](https://www.mapcamera.com/ec/assessment/items?genpinId=2984454), [R8 kit 買取](https://www.mapcamera.com/ec/assessment/items?genpinId=2984455)

Undated, and search snippet only — [uridoki 1](https://uridoki.net/pix/items/8afa78e2-5243-4493-a9c1-d9f99ed6baf8), [uridoki 2](https://uridoki.net/pix/items/a43a8f90-f63c-4620-930e-8ac7143b30ee), [uridoki 3](https://uridoki.net/pix/items/a59de325-4de6-48b7-ab0d-424229bf878d)

### Inferences
- Upgrade cost from R8 to R8 Mark II: ¥195,795 effective (lowest listing minus cashback) less about ¥105,000–120,000 resale ≈ **¥76,000–91,000 net**.
  - This assumes the aggregator figures hold.
  - Dealer trade-in boosts (下取 UP) could improve it. Fujiya's page mentions a "下取10%UP" condition.
